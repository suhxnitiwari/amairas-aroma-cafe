Our menu

Crafted with care

Every item is made with high-quality ingredients and thoughtful flavor combinations. Tap + on anything to add it to your order below.

At Amaira's Aroma Cafe, every drink begins with intention. We use house-crafted syrups, thoughtfully layered flavors, and seasonal ingredients to create lattes that feel both comforting and original. Our menu blends classic cafe staples with creative signature drinks inspired by warmth, nostalgia, and modern design. We prioritize balance over excess sweetness, so each flavor feels refined and approachable.

Many of our drinks come with dairy-free options and lighter alternatives without compromising texture or taste. Rather than mass-produced recipes, our drinks are built around consistency, clean presentation, and a calm cafe experience. Whether you're here for a familiar vanilla latte or a seasonal specialty, our goal is a cup that feels curated, cozy, and unmistakably Amaira.
