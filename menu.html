The menu

Poured with intention.

Ten signature lattes, fresh bakes and comforting plates. Tap + on anything to start your order.

At Amaira's Aroma Cafe, every drink begins with intention. We use house-crafted syrups, thoughtfully layered flavors and seasonal ingredients to create lattes that feel both comforting and original. Our menu blends classic cafe staples with creative signature drinks inspired by warmth, nostalgia and modern design.

We prioritize balance over excess sweetness, and many drinks come with dairy-free options without compromising texture or taste. Whether you're here for a familiar vanilla latte or a seasonal specialty, our goal is a cup that feels curated, cozy and unmistakably Amaira.
